Late Fee Rules

Below ₱100

No additional late charge. The applicable base fee still applies.

₱100 – ₱1,999

First 4 hours of each 24-hour period are FREE. Each completed 24-hour day has a ₱2 day penalty plus 20 chargeable hours at ₱0.50 per hour. Extra hours after the 4 free hours are ₱0.50 per hour. Minutes are ignored.

₱2,000 and above

Day 1 Late = ₱50. Day 2 onwards = ₱20 per completed day. No hourly penalty.
